How can I book an appointment at RV Hospital in Greater Noida?

You can request an appointment through the RV Hospital website. Enter your name, mobile number and required care area in the appointment form, then submit the request to contact the hospital on WhatsApp at +91 70650 10505. The hospital team can guide you regarding the appropriate department and appointment availability.

Where is RV Hospital located in Bisrakh, Greater Noida?

RV Hospital is located in Bisrakh, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Patients can use the Location section on this website to view the Google Maps location and get directions.

What medical services and specialties are available at RV Hospital?

RV Hospital's website currently highlights General Medicine, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Gynecology, Pediatrics, General Surgery, ENT, Dermatology, and Radiology & Diagnostics. Availability of a particular consultant or service can vary, so patients should confirm the required specialty before visiting.

Does RV Hospital provide emergency care 24/7?

For urgent medical situations, use the hospital's emergency contact shown on this website and call +91 70650 10505. Emergency availability, services and response arrangements should be confirmed directly with RV Hospital before relying on this as a final service claim.

What should I bring for my first visit to RV Hospital?

Bring a valid photo ID and any relevant previous prescriptions, medical reports, diagnostic reports or imaging records. If you are using health insurance, also carry your insurance or TPA documents where applicable.

Does RV Hospital offer diagnostic services in Greater Noida?

Can I contact RV Hospital on WhatsApp for an appointment enquiry?

Does RV Hospital provide specialist consultations for patients from Greater Noida West and nearby areas?
